Study Easy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Study Easy ("we," "us," "our," or "the Service") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

2. What Data We Collect

When you use Study Easy, we collect:

  • Google Calendar access: Your calendar events (read-only, except for study sessions we create on your behalf). We use this to identify when you're busy, so we can schedule study sessions in your free time.
  • Study goals: The subjects you want to study and weekly time targets you set.
  • Availability settings: Your daily study window, which days you study, session length preferences, timezone, and travel time rules.
  • Session history: Records of scheduled sessions and whether you marked them as done, partial, or skipped (so we can carry shortfalls into next week).
  • Account info: Your email address and Google account ID, for authentication only.

We do not collect, sell, or share:

  • The content of your calendar events (titles, descriptions, attendees, etc.) — we only see busy/free status and timing.
  • Any personal data beyond what you explicitly provide or what we read from Google Calendar.
  • Your data with third parties, except as required by law.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Scheduling: To run the scheduling algorithm and propose study sessions that fit your calendar.
  • Writing sessions: To create study events on your Google Calendar (in the calendar you select).
  • Tracking progress: To show you your 8-week study history and calculate carryover between weeks.
  • Service improvement: To fix bugs and improve the Service (we may review error logs, never including your personal content).

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Neon (a cloud database provider). Access is restricted to the Study Easy backend service only. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

5. Google Calendar Data

Study Easy uses Google Calendar API under Google's terms:

  • We request read access to your calendars (to see busy/free time) and write access to one calendar (to create study sessions).
  • Your Google Calendar data is never shared with anyone but you.
  • You can revoke Study Easy's access to your Google account at any time via myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You can download or request a copy of your data anytime.
  • Delete: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. Study sessions already on your calendar will remain unless you remove them yourself.
  • Correct: You can update your settings, goals, and session records.
  • Opt-out: You can revoke access to Google Calendar, which stops new sessions from being created (existing sessions stay on your calendar).

7. Security

We use industry-standard security practices:

  • HTTPS encryption for all connections.
  • Secure token storage (JWT with secret key).
  • Database access restricted to the backend service.
  • No passwords stored (we use Google OAuth, so your password is never shared with us).

If we discover a data breach, we will notify you as soon as possible.

8. Cookies & Tracking

Study Easy uses only essential cookies:

  • Authentication token: Stored in browser local storage to keep you logged in.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics or similar services.

9. Children

Study Easy is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that a child has provided personal information, we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy here and updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service means you accept the updated policy.
